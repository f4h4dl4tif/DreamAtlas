How to install DreamAtlas
=========================

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon Macs only (M1 or
newer); it won't open on Intel Macs. The Liquid Glass theme needs macOS 26
or later.

DreamAtlas isn't notarized by Apple, so on a new Mac macOS blocks it the
first time. Install it as below, and use the Terminal fixes if it fails.


INSTALL: Disk image (DreamAtlas-x.x-AppleSilicon.dmg)
-----------------------------------------------------
1. Open the .dmg and drag DreamAtlas onto the Applications folder.
2. Open DreamAtlas from Applications. If macOS says it "can't be opened" or
   "could not verify" it: click Done (don't move it to the Bin), open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click
   "Open Anyway" next to DreamAtlas. Enter your password.


IF IT STILL FAILS: Terminal commands
------------------------------------
Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste a command, press
Return and type your Mac password when asked (nothing shows as you type).

1. DreamAtlas is installed but won't open, or macOS says it is "damaged":

       sudo xattr -cr /Applications/DreamAtlas.app && open /Applications/DreamAtlas.app

2. "Open Anyway" doesn't appear, or the .dmg itself is blocked:

       xattr -c ~/Downloads/DreamAtlas-*

   then double-click the file again.

3. The morning reminder never shows, or Touch ID unlock keeps failing:

       tccutil reset All app.dreamatlas.mac

   then quit and reopen DreamAtlas, turn the reminder on again in
   Settings > Capture and allow notifications
   (System Settings > Notifications > DreamAtlas).

4. Check the Mac is new enough (must say 14 or higher):

       sw_vers -productVersion


AFTER INSTALLING
----------------
DreamAtlas opens on your journal. Press Option-Command-D from any app to jot
a dream down in the quick-capture panel, even while the journal is locked.
Settings (Command-,) has the morning reminder and "open at login" (Capture),
the Liquid Glass theme (Appearance, macOS 26+), the Touch ID or password lock
(Privacy; Control-Command-L locks right away), and export and backup (Data).
The free version holds 15 dreams; a lifetime license unlocks unlimited
dreams. DreamAtlas never connects to the internet.

Your journal is saved in ~/Library/Application Support/DreamAtlas
(journal.json). Back it up from Settings > Data before moving to another Mac.

To uninstall (this also deletes your dreams and your license key, so back up
first): quit DreamAtlas from its menu, then run

       rm -rf /Applications/DreamAtlas.app ~/Library/Application\ Support/DreamAtlas && defaults delete app.dreamatlas.mac
